# Example: daily glucose of patients with type 1 diabetes

Synthetic data for the **Time Series Forecasting** automator (generated by `make_example.py`).
Upload `Train.csv` and `Test.csv` on the automator's upload page; both are also downloadable from that page.

| Column | Role | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `ID` | series | patient (P001-P040) |
| `Time` | time | day (2024-01-01 to 2024-07-31) |
| `Target` | to forecast | daily mean glucose (mg/dL) |
| `Insulin_units` | known in advance (future) | prescribed daily insulin |
| `Weekend` | known in advance (future) | 1 on Saturdays and Sundays |
| `Steps` | observed up to now (past) | daily step count from a wearable; yesterday's activity lowers glucose |
| `Age`, `Sex`, `BMI` | static | patient characteristics |

- **Train.csv**: 30 patients followed for 100-160 days (3,901 rows).
- **Test.csv**: the next 14 days of these 30 patients (temporal hold-out), and 10 new patients with 60 days of
  history followed by the 14 days to forecast (unseen series).

Suggested settings: horizon 14, seasonal period 7, `Insulin_units` and `Weekend` known in advance, `Steps` observed
up to now. Every network should beat the seasonal naive baseline (MASE below 1), and the integrated gradients should
show the Target history, `Weekend` and `Steps` as the main inputs.
