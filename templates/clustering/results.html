About these metrics
Silhouette
From -1 to 1: how much closer each sample is to its own cluster than to the nearest other one. Above 0.5 is a strong structure, around 0.2 a weak one. Noise samples are left out.
Calinski-Harabasz
Between-cluster over within-cluster dispersion (higher is better); compare it between algorithms on the same data only.
Davies-Bouldin
Average similarity of each cluster with its most similar one (0 or more, lower is better).
{% if info.supervised %}
ARI / AMI / NMI
Agreement between the clusters and the Target classes: adjusted Rand index and (adjusted / normalised) mutual information. 0 is chance level, 1 a perfect match; they do not depend on how the clusters are numbered.
Homogeneity, completeness, V-measure
Whether each cluster holds a single class, whether each class falls in a single cluster, and their harmonic mean.
FMI, purity, accuracy
Fowlkes-Mallows index; share of samples in the majority class of their cluster; share of samples whose cluster maps to their class, clusters and classes matched one to one (Hungarian algorithm).
{% endif %}
Stability
ARI between the clusters that a model fitted on the other folds gives to the held-out samples and those of the model fitted on all of Train.csv: are the clusters reproducible?
Noise
Density-based algorithms (DBSCAN, HDBSCAN, OPTICS) leave samples outside every cluster (cluster -1).
