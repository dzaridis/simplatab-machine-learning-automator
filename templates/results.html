About these metrics
AUC
Area under the ROC curve: how well the model ranks positives above negatives, independently of the threshold (one-vs-rest average for multiclass).
Balanced accuracy
Average of the per-class recalls; not inflated by class imbalance.
F-score
Harmonic mean of precision and sensitivity.
Sensitivity / specificity
Share of positives detected / share of negatives correctly rejected (averaged over classes for multiclass).
Internal vs external
K-fold metrics come from the validation folds of {{ train_name }}; test metrics from {{ test_name }}, never seen during training or tuning.
