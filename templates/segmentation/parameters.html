Networks trained by the automator

Patches sampled from the {{ 'volumes resampled to the median spacing' if is3d else 'images' }} (a third centred on a structure), Dice + cross-entropy loss, a fixed number of epochs of a fixed number of iterations (as nnU-Net). The patch size is planned from your data.

Pretrained networks use 30% of the learning rate. Lower the batch size if the GPU runs out of memory.

Data augmentation

Random variations of the training patches; the masks follow the geometric ones.

nnU-Net schedule

nnU-Net trains for 1000 epochs of 250 iterations by default (about a day per fold on a GPU); a shorter schedule gives most of its accuracy in a fraction of the time.

Image preparation and uncertainty
{% if not summary.rgb %}

CT: intensities clipped to the 0.5–99.5 percentiles of the structures and standardised with their statistics (as nnU-Net). Z-score: each image standardised on its own (MRI, X-ray, microscopy). Automatic uses CT for CT series.

{% endif %}
Each image is also predicted flipped along each axis; the averaged prediction is more accurate and the disagreement between the passes gives the uncertainty map shown with the results (where boundaries are unreliable). Inference takes 4 times longer.
