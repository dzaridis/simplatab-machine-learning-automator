    Data requirements

    • Two zip files, at most {{ max_gb }} GB each, each with an images/ and a masks/ folder whose paths mirror each other
    • 2D: PNG, JPEG, TIFF, BMP (medical or natural images), DICOM, NIfTI
    • 3D: NIfTI volumes, multi-frame DICOM, or one folder per case with a DICOM series or several series (e.g. t2/, adc.nii.gz), used as channels
    • Masks: label images (0 = background; 0/255 for binary masks), palette or colour PNGs, NIfTI label maps
    • Class names in classes.txt (1,liver) or labels.json; the nnU-Net layout (imagesTr/, labelsTr/, dataset.json) also works
    • Sub-folders (one per patient) keep a patient's cases in the same validation fold

    Example layouts

    2D images
    Train.zip
├── images/
│   ├── case_001.png

└── masks/
    ├── case_001.png   (or case_001_mask.png)

    3D volumes
    Train.zip
├── images/
│   ├── case_01.nii.gz
│   └── case_02/          (one or more series)
│       ├── t2/           (DICOM slices)
│       └── adc.nii.gz
└── masks/
    ├── case_01.nii.gz
    └── case_02.nii.gz
    A 3D mask can be on the grid of the first series or anywhere in patient space: it is aligned on the image.
