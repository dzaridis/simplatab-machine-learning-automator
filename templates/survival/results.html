About these metrics
C-index
Share of comparable patient pairs whose predicted risks are in the order of their event times (Harrell). 0.5 is chance, 1 perfect; 0.7 is typical of good clinical models.
Uno's C-index
The C-index weighted by the inverse probability of censoring, up to the end of the evaluation window: less dependent on the censoring pattern.
IBS
Integrated Brier score: squared error of the predicted survival probabilities, averaged from the 10th to the 90th percentile of the event times (0 is perfect). Compare with the Kaplan-Meier reference, which ignores the features.
AUC@t, Brier@t
At each horizon t: how well the risk separates patients with an event before t from those event-free at t (time-dependent AUC), and the Brier score of the survival probability at t.
Censoring
Patients without the event at their last follow-up count until then; the metrics weight them by the probability of remaining under follow-up (IPCW).

For each model: the test patients split into low, intermediate and high risk by the tertiles of the training risks, with their Kaplan-Meier curves and log-rank test; the predicted survival of five test patients across the range of risk; and the calibration of the predicted survival at {{ '%g' % info.calibration_horizon }} (groups of patients, predicted vs. observed).
