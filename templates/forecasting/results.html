About these metrics
MAE / RMSE
Mean absolute error / root mean squared error, in the unit of the Target, over all the forecast points. RMSE weighs large errors more.
sMAPE
Symmetric mean absolute percentage error (0–200%): the error relative to the size of the values.
MASE
The error of each series divided by the error of the seasonal naive forecast on its history, averaged over the series. Below 1, the model beats repeating the last cycle.
Validation vs. test
Validation: the last {{ info.k_folds }} horizon(s) of every Train.csv series, each forecast by a network trained on the points before it (also used to tune). Test: the last {{ info.horizon }} points of every Test.csv series, forecast by the final networks trained on all of Train.csv.
