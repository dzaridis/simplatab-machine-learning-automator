Validation and training

Prequential validation on Train.csv: the last K horizons of every series are forecast one after the other, each by a network trained on the points before it. Errors are averaged over the windows.

Extra configurations tried per model (lookback, learning rate, size), each on all the windows; the one with the lowest mean validation MAE is kept. 0 uses the defaults. Each configuration costs one training per window.
