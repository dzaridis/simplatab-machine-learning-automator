    Data requirements

    • Long format: one row per series and time point
    • ID (or patient_id): the series, e.g. a patient
    • Time: dates at a regular frequency (daily, weekly, monthly…) or integer steps (visit 1, 2, 3…); missing time points are filled in
    • Target: the numeric value to forecast
    • Other columns are covariates: constant within a series → static feature; varying → known in advance or observed only up to now (you choose on the next page)
    • Test.csv: its last H points per series are forecast from the points before. Same IDs as Train.csv: the series continue (later period). New IDs: unseen series, with their own history.
