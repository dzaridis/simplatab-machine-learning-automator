    Data requirements

    • Two zip files, at most {{ max_gb }} GB each, with the images and one annotation format
    • 2D: DICOM, NIfTI, PNG (8 or 16-bit), JPEG, BMP, TIFF
    • 3D: NIfTI volumes, multi-frame DICOM or one folder of DICOM slices per series, with 3D boxes (CSV) or NIfTI masks
    • Boxes in pixels (voxels) of the original image; images without boxes are negatives
    • Sub-folders (one per patient) keep a patient's images in the same validation fold
