How it works

A complete, reproducible pipeline

What the tabular automator does after you press Run. The image automator follows the same steps, with medical image preparation, pretrained networks and Grad-CAM; the forecasting automator with rolling-origin validation and integrated gradients; the detection automator with pretrained detectors, K-fold or hold-out validation and D-RISE.

STEP 1

Check the data

Validates the target and the columns, removes incomplete rows and, if you ask, assesses bias with respect to a chosen feature.

STEP 2

Select and prepare features

Drops highly correlated features, keeps the most informative ones, standardises numeric and one-hot encodes categorical features.

STEP 3

Train and validate

Tunes each model with stratified K-fold cross-validation and finds the decision threshold that optimises your chosen metric.

STEP 4

Test and explain

Retrains on all of Train.csv, evaluates on the unseen Test.csv and explains each model's predictions with SHAP.

What you get

Everything needed to report a model, in one run
