Each fold holds out 10% of its training images to stop when they stop improving; the final network is then trained on all of Train.zip for the median best number of epochs. Lower the batch size if the GPU runs out of memory.

Data augmentation

Random variations of the training images, so the network does not memorise them. All images are also randomly cropped by up to 20%.
