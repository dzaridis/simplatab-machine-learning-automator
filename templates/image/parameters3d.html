Each fold holds out 10% of its training studies to stop when they stop improving; the final network is then trained on all of Train.zip for the median best number of epochs. 3D networks need much more memory than 2D ones: lower the batch size or the volume size if the GPU runs out of memory.

3D data augmentation

Random variations of the training volumes, so the network does not memorise them.
